TITLE
-----
[BETA] Kenshi Together - Co-op Multiplayer


SHORT SUMMARY (first line people see / for the store tagline)
-------------
Play Kenshi with your friends: one shared world, one squad tab per player, joined over Steam with no port forwarding.


DESCRIPTION
-----------
[h1]KENSHI TOGETHER — CO-OP MULTIPLAYER[/h1]

[b]One world. Separate squads. Shared suffering.[/b]

We're a few friends who wanted to experience the world of Kenshi together. So we decided to build the co-op mod we wished existed. We figured we probably weren't the only ones, so we decided to share it with everyone.

[b]Kenshi Together is currently in BETA and actively being developed.[/b] Expect bugs, regular updates and improvements as we continue playing and building alongside the community.

[h2]FEATURES[/h2]

[list]
[*]Explore and survive together in one shared world.
[*]Control your own characters and independent squads.
[*]Synchronized combat, NPCs, items, trading, buildings and research.
[*]Shared economy and persistent world using the host's save.
[*]Join through Steam friends. No IPs or port forwarding.
[*]In-game F2 panel for multiplayer controls, pings and troubleshooting.
[/list]

[h2]INSTALLATION[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]1.[/b] Subscribe to Kenshi Together on the Workshop.
[*][b]2.[/b] Install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kenshi/mods/847]RE_Kenshi 0.3.1+[/url] separately (required plugin loader).
[*][b]3.[/b] Enable Kenshi Together in your Kenshi mod list.
[*][b]4.[/b] Make sure everyone has the same mod versions and Steam is online.
[/list]

[h2]HOW TO PLAY[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]HOST:[/b] Start a new game → Press F2 → Host for friends.
[*][b]JOIN:[/b] Stay at the main menu → Press F2 → Join → Select your friend's session.
[*]The host's world loads automatically. Each player controls their own squad.
[/list]

[h2]BETA & FEEDBACK[/h2]

Currently tested with up to 3 players. Bugs and desyncs may happen!

[list]
[*][b]Desync?[/b] F2 → Resync. Give reality a gentle shove.
[*][b]Found a bug?[/b] F2 → Report. Send us the evidence and we'll track down the culprit.
[*][b]Got ideas?[/b] Drop them in the Workshop discussions. Even the terrible ones. This is Kenshi, after all.
[/list]

[h2]CREDITS & TRANSPARENCY[/h2]

Built upon [url=https://github.com/nhoral/KenshiCoop]KenshiCoop by nhoral[/url]. Huge thanks for laying the foundation that made this project possible!

[b]Developed entirely with AI.[/b] We're just gamers and game development enthusiasts learning the craft as we go. AI helped us turn an idea into something we could actually play, test and share.

We know AI isn't everyone's thing, and that's perfectly fair. Roast us, skip the mod, or give it a shot. No hard feelings either way. The technology is here, whether we like it or not. We figured we'd have more fun exploring what we can build with it than arguing about whether it should exist.
